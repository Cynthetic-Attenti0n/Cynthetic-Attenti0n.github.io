About Little Lifesavers

Little Lifesavers is a comprehensive pediatric first aid course designed specifically for parents and caregivers of infants and toddlers in Australia. Based on the latest guidelines from St John Ambulance Australia and the Australian Resuscitation Council, our course provides essential knowledge and skills to respond confidently in emergency situations.

Our interactive, modular approach makes learning accessible and engaging, with practical demonstrations, scenario-based learning, and regular assessments to reinforce your knowledge.

Interactive Learning

Engage with animations, simulations, and hands-on activities to build practical skills.

Flexible Timing

Learn at your own pace with short, focused modules that fit into your busy schedule.

Evidence-Based

Content regularly updated to align with the latest Australian first aid guidelines.
