# CASP (Critical Appraisal Skills Programme) Checklist for Qualitative Studies

## Overview
The CASP Qualitative Checklist is designed to help researchers systematically assess the quality and rigor of qualitative research. It provides a structured approach to critically appraise qualitative studies through a series of targeted questions.

## Key Questions in the CASP Checklist

### Section A: Validity of the Research
1. **Was there a clear statement of the aims of the research?**
   - Is the research question clearly defined?
   - Is the importance and relevance of the study explained?

2. **Is a qualitative methodology appropriate?**
   - Does the research seek to interpret or illuminate actions and/or subjective experiences of participants?
   - Is qualitative research the right methodology for addressing the research goal?

3. **Was the research design appropriate to address the aims of the research?**
   - Has the researcher justified the research design?
   - Has the researcher discussed how they decided which method to use?

4. **Was the recruitment strategy appropriate to the aims of the research?**
   - Has the researcher explained how participants were selected?
   - Has the researcher explained why the participants selected were the most appropriate?
   - Is there discussion around recruitment and potential bias?

5. **Was the data collected in a way that addressed the research issue?**
   - Is the setting for data collection justified?
   - Is it clear how data were collected?
   - Has the researcher justified the methods chosen?
   - Has the researcher discussed saturation of data?

6. **Has the relationship between researcher and participants been adequately considered?**
   - Has the researcher critically examined their own role, potential bias, and influence?
   - How has the researcher responded to events during the study?

### Section B: Results of the Study
7. **Have ethical issues been taken into consideration?**
   - Are there sufficient details of how the research was explained to participants?
   - Has approval been sought from an ethics committee?
   - Has the researcher discussed issues raised by the study?

8. **Was the data analysis sufficiently rigorous?**
   - Is there an in-depth description of the analysis process?
   - Is it clear how categories/themes were derived from the data?
   - Has the researcher explained how the data presented were selected?
   - Is sufficient data presented to support the findings?
   - Has the researcher critically examined their own role and potential bias during analysis?

9. **Is there a clear statement of findings?**
   - Are the findings explicit?
   - Is there adequate discussion of the evidence both for and against the researcher's arguments?
   - Has the researcher discussed the credibility of their findings?
   - Are the findings discussed in relation to the original research question?

### Section C: Relevance of the Study
10. **How valuable is the research?**
    - Does the researcher discuss the contribution the study makes to existing knowledge?
    - Does the researcher identify new areas where research is necessary?
    - Has the researcher discussed whether or how the findings can be transferred to other populations?

## Application Process
1. Answer each question with "Yes," "No," or "Can't Tell"
2. Provide supporting evidence for each answer
3. Consider the implications of any methodological weaknesses
4. Make an overall judgment about the quality of the study

## Implementation
The assessment is typically conducted by researchers or reviewers who are familiar with qualitative research methods. The checklist is not designed to generate a score but rather to guide critical thinking about the quality and value of qualitative research.
